MATRIX · PRIVACY

Privacy

Short version: this site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and your camera, microphone and photos never leave your device. It keeps a few settings in your own browser, and the Live board fetches weather and GitHub data from three public APIs.

LAST UPDATED 2026-11-04

Who runs this

Matrix is a personal, non-commercial project by Yash Mahajan. The source code is public at github.com/YashM20/dot-matrix. There is no backend, no database and nothing to sign in to: the site is a set of static files, and everything runs in your browser.

What the site collects

Nothing personal. There are no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no fingerprinting and no third-party fonts or CDNs (the typeface is served from this site). The page's Content Security Policy only allows connections to this site and to the APIs listed below.

What is stored on your device

Some settings and the things you make are saved in your browser's localStorage for this site, so they survive a reload. Every key below stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The site works without any of them, and most are only written once you change a setting or make something.

Settings

dot480:theme
The colour scheme, accent and contrast you picked, and for a custom accent its hue and the colours made from it.
dot480:share-theme
Whether links you copy carry your theme.
dot480:cards
The card size you picked (large, medium or small).
dot480:skin
The display skin you picked (OLED, CRT, e-ink and so on).
dot480:pro
Pro mode is on.
dot480:flags
Feature switches you turned on or off in this browser, such as the flip-disc clack sound.
dot480:clack, dot480:worker
Older copies of two of those switches. They are still read, and removed the next time you change the switch.
dot480:intro
You closed the one-line intro above the boards.
dot480:fold:…
Which "More" panels in MAKE you left open or closed, one key per panel.
dot480:pond
Only if you turn on POND MEMORY: the day the pond was first made, the day of your last visit and a visit count, so the pond can age. Turning it off deletes it.

Things you make

dot480.dots.gallery
Up to 40 DOTS programs you ran or remixed in MAKE · CODE.
dot480.studio.v1, dot480.studio.library
The card you are composing in MAKE · COMPOSE, and up to 24 cards you saved there.
dot480.editor.v1
The drawing board's document, saved as you work.
dot480.editor.clip
The last frames or layers you copied in the drawing board, so you can paste them in another tab.
dot480.editor.links
The last 40 drawing board links you made or opened: their id, name, size and time, so the board can show where a remix came from.
dot480.editor.sheet
On phones, how tall you left each of the drawing board's bottom panels.
dot480.hive.scores.v1
The game's high-score table: three-letter initials and scores.

Live data

dot480:live:…
The last weather, GitHub contributions and now-playing readings the Live board showed, one key per source, so it can start from them next time. They are ignored after 7 days.

One key lives in sessionStorage instead, which your browser clears when you close the tab:

dot480:tilt
You allowed tilt on this tab, so tilt-reactive cards don't ask again until you close it.

Open tabs of the site also talk to each other over a BroadcastChannel (so clocks and live cards stay in step); those messages never leave your browser and aren't stored.

To clear it all: remove this site's data in your browser settings (usually under site settings or "cookies and site data" for matrix.reactopia.me), or in developer tools under Application → Local storage and Session storage. In a private window it is gone when you close the window.

Camera, microphone and motion

Some cards can use your device's sensors. The camera and microphone are only requested after you press the card's control, and your browser asks you first:

  • Camera (the mirror cards): frames from the front camera are drawn into the page and turned into dots on the spot. They are not recorded, saved or uploaded, and the camera stops when you switch it off or leave the tab.
  • Microphone (the audio board): the sound is analysed in the page by the browser's audio engine to move the dots. It never plays through your speakers and is not uploaded.
  • Motion and tilt (tilt-reactive cards on phones): orientation readings only move the dots on screen.
  • Gamepad (the game): button presses are read while you play.

The site never asks for your location. If you use a REC or export button, the clip or image (a REC clip can include the board's sound) is made in your browser and saved to your device as a download; it is not uploaded. You can revoke any permission in your browser's site settings at any time.

Photos and files you open

An image you drop, paste or pick on the home page's touchable card, a photo you drop into "Your photo as dots" or use as a reference in DRAW, and any JSON card or drawing you open, is read and processed inside your browser. It is not uploaded anywhere, and the home card keeps nothing once you leave the page.

Share links

When you share a DOTS program, the program itself is packed into the link (the p= part of the address), and a drawing board link packs its frames the same way (the d= part). Nothing is stored on a server: anyone with the link can read the program, it ends up in browser history like any address, and the request for it appears in the hosting logs described below. Don't put anything private in a program you share. Copy buttons write to your clipboard only when you press them.

Third-party services

Only the Live board talks to other services, only while it is on screen, and the requests come straight from your browser. Like any website, each service sees your IP address and browser type when it is contacted.

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates to forecast, rounded to two decimals. These are Tokyo's by default, or the lat / lon you put in the address; the site does not read or send your location. See Open-Meteo's terms.
  • GitHub contributions (github-contributions-api.jogruber.de, a community service, not GitHub) receives the GitHub username whose public contribution calendar is shown: the author's by default, or the gh you put in the address.
  • GitHub (api.github.com) receives the same username, only if the service above fails, to read that user's public activity. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to other sites (GitHub, the essay's references) are ordinary links; once you follow one, that site's own policy applies. The site tells them only that you came from this site, not which page.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes the technical details of each request (such as IP address, browser user agent, the address requested and the time) to deliver and protect the site, under Vercel's privacy policy. This project does not turn on Vercel's analytics and does not use those logs to identify visitors.

The MCP server and scripts

The mcp/ server and the scripts in the repository are not part of the website. They only run if you clone the repository and start them on your own computer; the MCP server talks to your AI client over stdio, and its push_to_device tool only sends to a local (loopback) address you configure.

Children

The site isn't aimed at children. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If what the site stores or sends changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. Every past version is in the repository's history.
