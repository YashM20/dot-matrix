01 ENTER · CHANGE bayer

Mask transition. Every dot flips exactly once, in the order its static mask gives it. The bright line is the scan edge. The masks cycle through bayer, radial and wipe dither, curtain, and IGN noise. The same function covers load-in, state change and the reduced-motion dissolve.

02 DATA CHANGE decode

Decode scramble and spring. Changed digits scramble, then resolve staggered from left to right. Unchanged digits hold. The bar runs on a closed-form critically damped spring (Holden), so it's a pure function of time and can be scrubbed. It's drawn on a coarse lattice because bigger elements use bigger dots.

03 INTERACTION hover · press · tab

The pointer warps what the dots sample; the dots never move. Hovering gives a Sarkar–Brown fisheye and a glow on idle dots. Click a row to press it: it drops one size step and springs back. Clicking also starts a ripple simulation that refracts what the dots sample. Row 1 is a toggle. You can also use Tab plus the ↑ ↓ keys and Enter; the focused row gets marching dots.

04 DEPTH torch light

Lambert-lit SDF heightfields. The light follows your pointer, and orbits on its own when the pointer is away. You get an embossed button, a dome, halftone shadows on a lit backplate, a 1-dot occlusion moat, and a stepped bloom around the accent LED. Everything stays one accent and quantized dots.
